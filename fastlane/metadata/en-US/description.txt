The app of iPhoneBlog.de, the independent German publication about Apple, the iPhone and the App Store: every article, the #one content for members, and the discussion under each article. The articles are in German.

ARTICLES
Every article of iPhoneBlog.de, as on the website, without signing in.

DISCUSSION
Under each article, "Diskussion" opens the readers' replies. Anyone can read them. Signed in, you reply right in the app, with pictures, reactions and mentions.

#ONE
The content for #one members: #one articles, videos and the podcast. With it comes a room where members talk about the news, with threads, pictures, voice messages and notifications for new replies and @mentions.

SIGN IN
With your iphoneblog.de account. The app has no password of its own.

RESPECTFUL
Every message and every person can be reported and blocked. iPhoneBlog.de's moderation looks at every report.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything runs on our own server in Germany.
